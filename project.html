Location
Comporta, Portugal
Year
2026
Type
Residential
Area
420 m²

Project narrative

A house drawn between horizon and shelter.

Casa Vetra is imagined as a sequence of shaded thresholds facing the Atlantic landscape. Instead of treating the ocean as a view to be framed, the project allows light, wind and reflection to shape the experience of each room.

The material palette is deliberately restrained: pale mineral surfaces, darkened timber, oxidized metal and cool stone. A deep central void anchors the plan and creates a quiet contrast between openness and protection.
